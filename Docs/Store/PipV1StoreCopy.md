## App Name

## Subtitle

## Promotional Text

Make space for a personal Kegel and pelvic floor routine with local reminders and a short guided session.

## Description

Pip is a simple, private reminder for a personal Kegel and pelvic floor routine.

Choose up to three reminder times that follow your device time zone and Focus settings. When you are ready, Pip guides a short lift-and-release session with optional haptics. See today's completed sessions and your current streak at a glance.

Your information stays on your device. Pip has no account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, in-app purchases, or HealthKit connection.

Pip is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Stop if you have pain or concerns and consult a qualified healthcare professional.
