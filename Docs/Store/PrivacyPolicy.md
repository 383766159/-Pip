# Pip Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Pip is a private Kegel and pelvic floor reminder. This policy describes how Pip handles information.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Pip stores your completed session count and reminder choices locally on your device. Pip's widget and companion surfaces use local information only to display your Pip status.

If you grant notification permission, Pip schedules local notifications on your device at the reminder times you choose. You can change reminders in Pip and manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Pip does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Pip has no account, backend service, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, in-app purchases, HealthKit connection, or third-party tracking.

## Your Choices

You can turn reminders on or off, change reminder times, and manage notifications in iOS Settings. Removing Pip from your device removes its local app data, subject to iOS system behavior.

Because Pip does not transmit or retain personal information on a server, there is no account or server-side data to request or delete. To remove the locally stored Pip data, delete Pip from your device. You can revoke notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

## Changes to This Policy

If Pip's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the related app release.
